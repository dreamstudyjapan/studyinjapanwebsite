日本語学科 概要

長年にわたり外国人の日本語教育に携わり、 優秀な人材を輩出。

For many years, we have been dedicated to teaching Japanese to foreigners, helping to cultivate exceptional talent.

WCGB offers language courses for international students who want to learn Japanese in order to enroll in universities, graduate schools and vocational schools or to find a job in Japan.

For more details please visit: https://www.wcgb.ac.jp/japanese-outline/
